## Key decisions

### Localized by default, not as an afterthought

Settings include **English, Yoruba, Hausa, and Igbo**, plus live
currency conversion across **NGN, USD, EUR, and GBP** — the product is
built for a Nigerian user base rather than adapted for one after the
fact.

### The room isn't just a photo carousel

Room detail screens use **tappable hotspots** on the room photo (tap
"Bathroom" or "Living Room" to see labeled details), giving a more
concrete sense of the space than a standard image gallery.

### Booking includes the parts that actually cause friction

The booking form has a built-in **airport pickup add-on** (flight
number, arrival time) and the payment step supports **card, bank
transfer, and USSD** — reflecting real local payment behavior rather
than defaulting to card-only.

### Every listing is instantly shareable

A "Share this Service" action generates a direct link to a listing for
WhatsApp, Facebook, or Instagram — turning discovery into something a
user can hand off to someone else, not just book solo.
