## Approach

a walkthrough of what studying abroad through the platform actually
looks like, and a low-friction way to stay in touch before committing.

## Key decisions

### Leading with trust, not features

The hero pairs the core promise ("Your Global Study Journey Starts
Here") with a **verified-partner badge** and direct routing to
"Apply Directly to Verified Partner Schools" &mdash; addressing the
trust gap before anything else on the page.

### Two visual directions tested side by side

The design was explored as two distinct color/layout variants rather
than settling on one direction early &mdash; a deliberate way to
resolve the client's back-and-forth through comparison rather than
description.
