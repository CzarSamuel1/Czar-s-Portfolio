## Approach

MickkyStore is organized around distinct operational modules —

## Key decisions

### One dashboard, not a pile of tools

The Super Admin dashboard surfaces staff count, total sales, open and
completed repairs, and recent activity in one view, with quick-access
tiles into Accounting, Inventory, Cross Messaging, and Repair Tracking
— built so a manager can see the state of the business without
switching between systems.

### Repairs tracked like support tickets, not a spreadsheet row

Each repair carries the customer's details, device, and **serial/IMEI
number**, the logged issue, the assigned engineer, a status
(e.g. *In Progress*), expected completion date, and diagnosis notes —
closer to a support-ticket workflow than a simple repairs list, which
matters for accountability across branches.

### Staff accountability is branch-aware, not just role-based

The Team Space module ties attendance and payroll to branch location —
a staff-location map, Early/Late attendance status, and a full payroll
flow (salary, bonus, deductions, net salary) — reflecting that a
multi-branch operation needs to know not just *who* did something, but
*where*.
